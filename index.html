📜 CoinRush Pro — Game Rules

🎯 1. General Rules

CoinRush Pro is a reward-based task and quiz platform. Users can complete available activities and earn coins.

👤 2. Account Rules

• One account should be used by one user.
• Do not create duplicate or fake accounts.
• Do not share your password.
• Suspicious activity may result in account review.

🎁 3. Daily Bonus

• Once per day.
• Reward: 100 coins.

📺 4. Watch Ads

• Available ad tasks can be completed.
• Reward: 10 coins per completed task.
• Daily limits may apply.

🧠 5. Quiz Rules

• Math + GK questions.
• Maximum 40 correct questions per day.
• Correct answer: 10 coins.
• Wrong answers do not increase the daily counter.
• Quiz locks after 40 correct answers.

📋 6. Survey Center

• Reward: 25 coins per completed survey.
• Maximum 20 surveys/day.

🃏 7. Lucky Card

• Once per day.
• Possible rewards: 30, 50, 75, 100 coins.

🎟️ 8. Promocode

• Active promocode is controlled by the administrator.
• One promotional reward can be claimed per day.

📱 9. Download Tasks

• Up to 10 Play Store task options can be displayed each day.
• Tasks open the selected Play Store listing.
• Coin reward requires a genuine, verifiable completion mechanism; opening the store alone is not treated as an installation.

👥 10. Referral

• Each user gets a referral link based on account ID.
• Self-referrals and fake accounts are not allowed.

💎 11. Redeem

• Minimum: 9,000 coins.
• 9,000 coins = ₹30.
• Maximum: 20,000 coins/day.
• Valid UPI required.
• Processing: 24–72 hours.

🚫 12. Prohibited Activities

• Bots
• Fake accounts
• Duplicate activity
• Coin manipulation
• Bug exploitation
• False redemption information

🔐 13. Security

Keep your password private. Account and reward eligibility are subject to the application's rules and verification.
