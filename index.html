📜 CoinRush Pro — Game Rules

🎯 1. General Rules

CoinRush Pro is a reward-based task and quiz platform. Users can complete available activities and earn coins. Coins can be used for eligible withdrawals according to the withdrawal rules.

👤 2. Account Rules

• One account should be used by one user.
• Do not create duplicate or fake accounts.
• Do not share your password with anyone.
• Account information should be kept accurate.
• Suspicious or abusive activity may result in account review.

🎁 3. Daily Bonus

• Daily Bonus can be claimed once per day.
• Daily Bonus reward: 100 coins.
• After claiming, the bonus remains locked until the next day.

📺 4. Watch Ads

• Users can complete available advertisement tasks.
• Reward: 10 coins per completed task in this version.
• Daily task limits may apply.
• Do not use bots, auto-clickers or artificial activity.

🧠 5. Quiz Rules

• Quiz contains Math and GK questions.
• Questions are generated/selected from a large question pool.
• Maximum 40 questions per day.
• Correct answer reward: 10 coins.
• Wrong answer does not give coins.
• After 40 questions, Quiz becomes LOCKED for that day.
• Quiz limit resets on the next day.

📋 6. Survey Center

• Available survey tasks can be completed from Survey Center.
• Reward: 25 coins per completed survey in this version.
• Maximum 20 survey tasks per day.
• Survey availability may change.

🃏 7. Lucky Card

• Lucky Card can be used once per day.
• Possible rewards include 30, 50, 75 and 100 coins.
• Once used, it remains locked until the next day.

🎟️ 8. Daily Promocode

• A valid daily promocode can be claimed once per day.
• Daily promocode reward: 200 coins.
• One user cannot claim the same daily reward repeatedly.

📱 9. Download Task

• Download task can be completed once per day in this version.
• Reward: 20 coins.
• Repeated submissions may be rejected.

👥 10. Referral Rules

• Each user has a referral link based on their account ID.
• Referral links can be shared with friends.
• Referral rewards should only be given for genuine eligible referrals.
• Self-referrals and fake accounts are not allowed.

💸 11. Withdrawal Rules

• Minimum withdrawal: 9,000 coins.
• 9,000 coins = ₹30 in the current conversion.
• Maximum withdrawal: 20,000 coins per day.
• A valid UPI ID is required.
• Withdrawal requests are sent to the admin panel.
• Normal processing time: 24–72 hours.
• Admin can mark a request as Paid or Rejected.
• Incorrect UPI details can delay or prevent payment.

⏱️ 12. Withdrawal Processing

Every withdrawal request is recorded with the user's name, mobile/account ID, UID, coin amount, rupee amount, UPI ID, date and status for admin processing.

🚫 13. Prohibited Activities

• Bots or automated clicking
• Fake accounts
• Duplicate/fraudulent activity
• Manipulating coins or rewards
• Exploiting bugs intentionally
• False or misleading withdrawal information

⭐ 14. VIP Levels

VIP status is displayed according to the current CoinRush reward system. VIP-related benefits or limits may change when the reward system is updated.

🔐 15. Security & Account Safety

Keep your login credentials private. Do not share your password or account access. Coin balances and withdrawal eligibility are subject to the application's verification and reward rules.

⚠️ 16. Important

Coin rewards, task availability, limits and withdrawal processing may be updated by the CoinRush administrator. Always check the latest rules shown inside the application.
